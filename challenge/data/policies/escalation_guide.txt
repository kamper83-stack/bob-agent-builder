ACME CORP — ESCALATION & CONDUCT GUIDE
Version 1.3 | Effective: March 2025
[SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — FOR POC USE ONLY]

---

1. WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate immediately (do not wait for first response) when:
- Customer mentions legal action or regulatory complaint
- Same issue has been open for more than 72 hours
- Complaint involves a manager or senior staff member
- Customer requests to speak to a director or above

2. CONDUCT STANDARDS FOR SERVICE REPRESENTATIVES

Representatives are expected to:
- Use the customer's name at least once during each interaction
- Never interrupt a customer while they are explaining their issue
- Offer to repeat key points back to the customer to confirm understanding
- Avoid jargon, acronyms, or internal terminology
- Never place a customer on hold for more than 5 minutes without checking in

Violations of conduct standards must be reported to the representative's direct manager within 2 business hours of the complaint being received.

3. LANGUAGE GUIDELINES FOR WRITTEN RESPONSES

AVOID:
- "Unfortunately there is nothing we can do"
- "That is not our policy"
- "You should have read the terms"
- Passive-aggressive phrasing

USE INSTEAD:
- "I understand how frustrating this must be"
- "Let me find the best way to help you"
- "Here is what I can do for you right now"
- "I will personally follow up to make sure this is resolved"

4. FEEDBACK LOOP

After every complaint resolved, the handling manager rates the quality of the response:
1 = Needs immediate coaching | 3 = Meets standard | 5 = Exemplary
Ratings below 3 trigger an automatic coaching session booking.

5. DOCUMENTATION

Every complaint response and internal note must be logged in the CRM within 1 hour of sending.
Responses sent without CRM logging are a compliance violation.
