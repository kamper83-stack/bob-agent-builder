ACME CORP — CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICY
Version 2.1 | Effective: January 2025
[SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT — FOR POC USE ONLY]

---

1. CUSTOMER COMPLAINT HANDLING

1.1 All customer complaints must be acknowledged within 4 business hours of receipt.
1.2 The acknowledgment must include: (a) the customer's name, (b) a reference number, (c) a timeline for resolution.
1.3 Complaints involving rude or unprofessional conduct by a service representative are classified as Internal Conduct Complaints and must also trigger an internal action note to the representative's direct manager.
1.4 The response tone must be professional, empathetic and solution-oriented. Avoid defensive language.

2. SEVERITY LEVELS

LOW: Customer dissatisfied with wait time or process. Resolution target: 48 hours.
MEDIUM: Incorrect information given, or representative conduct issue. Resolution target: 24 hours.
HIGH: Repeated unresolved issue, threat to escalate publicly, or legal mention. Resolution target: 4 hours. Escalate to senior team lead immediately.

3. RESPONSE STANDARDS

3.1 Begin every response by thanking the customer for bringing the issue to our attention.
3.2 Acknowledge the specific issue raised — do not use generic templates.
3.3 State clearly what action will be taken and by when.
3.4 Offer a direct contact (name and email) for follow-up.
3.5 Close with an apology for the inconvenience, without admitting liability.

4. INTERNAL ACTION NOTE

When an Internal Conduct Complaint is filed:
- Note the complaint reference, date, representative ID, and nature of the complaint.
- State the action taken: coaching session scheduled / formal warning issued / no action (with reason).
- Manager must sign off within 24 hours.

5. ESCALATION

If the customer is not satisfied after the first response, escalate to the Customer Experience Manager.
Do not promise refunds or compensation without manager approval.
